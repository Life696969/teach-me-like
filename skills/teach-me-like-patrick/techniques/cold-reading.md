# Technique: Cold reading

_Character-free technique note. Source keys refer to `references/sources.md`._

## What it is

Cold reading is how someone seems to "know" things about a stranger without prior information. Fake psychics, fortune tellers and some mentalists use it. It mixes observation with **statements built to feel personal while fitting almost anyone**, and it reads the person's reactions to steer. Learning it does two things: it makes you a sharper reader, and it makes you **immune** to people who use it on you.

## The toolkit

1. **Barnum statements:** vague, flattering, two-sided lines that most people accept as true about themselves. Example: "You can be very sociable, but there are times you need to be alone to recharge." (Forer 1949.)
2. **Rainbow ruse:** naming a trait *and* its opposite so every outcome is a hit. "You're generous, but you've been burned for it, so now you're careful."
3. **High-probability guesses:** facts that are common for this person's age, place and situation. A 50-year-old has probably lost an older relative; a student is probably stressed about money or exams.
4. **Fishing:** questions disguised as statements. "I'm sensing a J or a G name…" and then letting the person fill it in.
5. **Shotgunning:** many quick guesses; build on the hits, quietly drop the misses. Audiences remember hits and forget misses.
6. **Reading reactions:** nods, leaning in, pauses, eye moistening. These tell the reader which direction is warm.
7. **Feedback looping:** repeating what the person just told you back to them later as if it were a revelation.

## How to practise it (honestly)

- Learn each move by **spotting** it first: watch a psychic reading video and tag every Barnum line, fish and shotgun.
- Then **build** a reading for a fictional person from a photo, labelling each line by technique.
- Only ever perform a reading **as a demonstration**, revealed afterwards ("Here's how I did that"). That is the ethical version, and it is also the most impressive part.

## Science check

- **Works:** the Barnum (Forer) effect is real and robust. In the original class demonstration, 39 students all received the same "personal" sketch, mostly copied from an astrology book, and rated it 4.26 out of 5 for accuracy (Forer 1949); it has replicated many times. Cold-reading methods are well documented by investigators of psychics (Hyman 1977; Rowland 1998). Hyman himself came to believe in palm reading until he tried giving the *opposite* readings and found they landed just as well.
- **Works:** selective memory and confirmation bias make shotgunning feel like mind reading.
- **TV exaggeration:** cold reading producing specific hidden facts (a name, an address) reliably. Real cold reading rarely produces specifics without fishing.
- **Myth:** that a good cold reader has a special perceptive gift. It is a learnable script plus audience psychology.

## Defence (how to resist it)

- Notice when *you* supplied the information.
- Ask "Would this be true for most people my age?"
- Count misses as carefully as hits.

## Ethics line

Never use cold reading to pretend to have psychic powers, to take money, or to manipulate someone grieving. Demonstrate it, then reveal it.

## Drill ideas

- **Barnum or not?** The agent gives 6 statements; the learner tags which are Barnum lines.
- **Write a reading** for a stock-photo person, with each line labelled by technique.
- **Spot the fish** in a short scripted psychic reading.
